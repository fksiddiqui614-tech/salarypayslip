Is $60,000 a Year a Good Salary?

A $60,000 salary sits above the US national average individual income and is much closer to the median US household income than $50,000 is. For most single earners, $60,000 is a comfortable salary in the majority of the country, though the same location factors still apply.

💡

Quick answer: $60,000 a year (about $28.85/hour at 40 hrs/week) is a comfortable salary for a single person in most US cities, and workable — though not roomy — for a family in lower-to-mid cost-of-living areas.

How $60,000 Compares to the US Average

$60,000 sits meaningfully above the average individual salary (~$48,000–$49,000), and much closer to — though still slightly below — the median household income of roughly $68,700–$80,600. (Source: US Bureau of Labor Statistics; US Census Bureau, American Community Survey) Unlike a $50,000 salary, $60,000 puts a single earner within striking distance of matching a typical two-earner household on their own.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is $60,000 a year a good salary for a single person?+

Yes, for a single person $60,000 a year is comfortable in most US cities, and only tight in the most expensive metro areas like New York or San Francisco.

Is $60,000 a year above the US average salary?+

Yes, $60,000 sits noticeably above the US average individual salary of roughly $48,000-$49,000, though it's still somewhat below the median household income.

What is $60,000 a year per hour?+

At a standard 40-hour workweek and 52 weeks a year, $60,000 a year is approximately $28.85 per hour before taxes.

Is $60,000 enough to support a family?+

It depends on location and family size, but $60,000 supports a small family more comfortably than $50,000 does, particularly in low-to-average cost-of-living areas.

In which states does $60,000 go the furthest?+

$60,000 stretches furthest in low cost-of-living states such as Oklahoma, Arkansas, Mississippi and Kansas, and is comfortable in most average cost-of-living states too.
