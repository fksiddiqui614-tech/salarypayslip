Convert Annual Salary to Hourly Rate

Our annual salary to hourly rate calculator helps you estimate your hourly equivalent from a yearly salary. Enter your annual salary, average hours worked per week and number of working weeks per year to get an estimated hourly rate.

For a standard full-time schedule, 40 hours per week and 52 weeks per year equals 2,080 working hours. You can change these values if your schedule is different.

How to Calculate Hourly Rate From Salary

To calculate an hourly rate from an annual salary, divide the annual salary by the total number of working hours in a year.

Hourly Rate = Annual Salary ÷ (Hours per Week × Weeks per Year)

For other common pay conversions, you can use these formulas:

Annual Salary → Hourly Rate: Annual Salary ÷ (Hours per Week × Weeks per Year)

Hourly Rate → Annual Salary: Hourly Rate × Hours per Week × Weeks per Year

Monthly Salary → Hourly Rate: (Monthly Salary × 12) ÷ (Hours per Week × Weeks per Year)

Weekly Pay → Hourly Rate: Weekly Pay ÷ Hours per Week

For example, if your annual salary is $60,000 and you work 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, the calculation is:

$60,000 ÷ (40 × 52) = $28.85 per hour

Annual Salary = Hourly Rate × Hours per Week × Weeks per Year

Salary and Hourly Pay Conversion

A salary and an hourly wage describe compensation in different ways. An annual salary provides a yearly amount, while an hourly rate shows the equivalent amount for each hour worked.

The actual equivalent can change when your working schedule changes. That is why this calculator lets you customize hours per week and weeks per year instead of forcing a single schedule.
