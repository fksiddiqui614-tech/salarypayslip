What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember user preferences, improve performance, and understand how visitors interact with different pages.

Cookies generally do not contain information that directly identifies an individual user. Instead, they help improve website functionality and provide a more consistent browsing experience.

Important Notice

Cookies are widely used across the internet to improve website usability, performance, and security.
