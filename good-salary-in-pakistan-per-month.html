What Is a Good Salary in Pakistan Per Month?

Many people in Pakistan wonder what is a good salary per month. With rising living costs, rent, education, and daily expenses, the definition of a “good salary” can vary depending on your profession, city, and lifestyle.

Average Salary in Pakistan

Based on multiple surveys and Google salary data, the average monthly salary in Pakistan ranges from PKR 35,000 to 82,000. Entry-level positions, informal work, and students usually earn on the lower side, while skilled professionals and corporate employees earn much more.

Salary for Freelancers

Freelancers earn based on skills and clients. Beginners may earn PKR 30,000 – 50,000/month, while experienced freelancers can exceed PKR 120,000.

Salary for Students and Interns

Students and interns usually earn PKR 20,000 – 50,000 per month.

Salary for Corporate Employees

Corporate employees' salaries vary by role, experience, and company size. Entry-level positions start around PKR 40,000 – 70,000, while senior management can earn PKR 200,000+.

Salary for Finance Professionals / Users

Finance professionals such as accountants and investment managers generally earn PKR 120,000 – 250,000+.

Good Salary by City

  • Karachi: PKR 100,000 – 150,000
  • Lahore: PKR 80,000 – 130,000
  • Islamabad: PKR 90,000 – 140,000
