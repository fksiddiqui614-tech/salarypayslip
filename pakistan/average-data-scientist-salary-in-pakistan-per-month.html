What Is the Average Data Scientist Salary in Pakistan Per Month?

A data scientist in Pakistan typically earns PKR 150,000 to PKR 500,000 per month. Pay depends mainly on whether the role is closer to reporting (data analyst work) or to building and deploying models (machine learning work), on the employer, and on whether the job is local or remote. This guide gives real ranges by experience, city and employer, explains what actually separates a data scientist's pay from a data analyst's, and includes an international salary comparison for readers checking pay outside Pakistan.

Data Scientist Salary in Pakistan: Quick Answer

Most people hired with the title "Data Scientist" in Pakistan earn PKR 150,000 to PKR 500,000 per month. In practice, the job market uses the title loosely: some "data scientist" roles are really data analyst work (dashboards, SQL, reporting) paying toward the lower end, while roles that involve building and deploying machine learning models pay toward the upper end and beyond, especially at fintechs and remote-first companies.

The jump from Data Scientist to Senior is the biggest one, and it usually requires having shipped at least one model into a real product, not just built it in a notebook. Many Pakistani data scientists get this experience by moving to a company that actually deploys ML in production, since many local "data science" roles never go past reporting and dashboards.

Data Scientist vs Data Analyst vs Machine Learning Engineer

These three titles are used inconsistently by Pakistani employers, and the pay difference usually tracks the actual work, not the title on the job ad.

RoleCore workMonthly salary (PKR)
Data AnalystSQL, dashboards (Power BI/Tableau), reporting on data that already exists90,000 – 250,000
Data ScientistBuilds statistical or ML models, tests hypotheses, works with analysts and engineers150,000 – 500,000
Machine Learning EngineerTakes a model and puts it into production: APIs, pipelines, monitoring200,000 – 600,000+

If a job ad titled "Data Scientist" lists only Excel, SQL and Power BI, it is a data analyst role by another name and will pay at the lower end. If it lists model deployment, MLOps, or production ML systems, expect pay closer to an ML engineer.

How Data Science Pay Actually Works in Pakistan

Unlike a doctor or engineer, "data scientist" is not a regulated title, so the pay gap between two people with the same title can be large. Employers are effectively paying for a mix of three things: coding ability (Python, SQL), statistical/ML knowledge, and the ability to turn a business question into a model that a non-technical manager can use. Candidates who are strong in only one of these three usually get offers at the lower half of the range.

In hiring, most companies test with a take-home case study or a live SQL/Python round rather than checking certificates, so a strong GitHub or Kaggle profile with 2-3 real projects usually moves an offer more than a certification does. Where a company already has a data platform in place (clean pipelines, existing dashboards), a data scientist there spends more time modelling; where they don't, the "data scientist" spends most of their time doing data engineering and analyst work for analyst-level pay.

Remote Data Science Work

Data science work is easier to do remotely than most jobs because the data, code and models all live in the cloud. Pakistani data scientists commonly work for US, UK and Gulf companies on contract or full-time remote roles, usually paid in dollars. The main filter for these roles is a strong portfolio (GitHub, published Kaggle results, or a couple of real production projects) plus comfortable English communication for client or team calls, since most remote hiring skips formal degree checks in favour of a technical interview.

The gap is not just cost of living. US, UK and Canadian employers pay more for data scientists partly because more companies there have mature ML infrastructure already in place, so the role is closer to the "senior, deploys models" end of the spectrum described earlier on this page, rather than the analyst-adjacent work common in many Pakistani roles. A Pakistani data scientist targeting one of these markets, whether by relocating or by working remotely for a foreign employer, is generally judged by the same bar: a portfolio of deployed models, not just notebooks, plus a credential check (a work visa for relocation, or a straightforward technical interview for remote contracts).

Within the region, India's data science market is larger and more mature than Pakistan's, with more specialised roles (NLP, computer vision, MLOps) as distinct job titles rather than folded into one generalist "data scientist" role. Pakistani data scientists competing for remote roles against Indian candidates are usually judged on the same portfolio-first basis described in the roadmap section below.

How to Get Into Data Science in Pakistan

  1. Learn Python and SQL properly before touching machine learning libraries.
  2. Study statistics and probability, since most beginners skip this and it shows up in interviews.
  3. Do 2-3 real projects with public datasets (not tutorials) and put the code and a write-up on GitHub.
  4. Learn one cloud platform well enough to deploy a simple model as an API.
  5. Apply for data analyst or junior data scientist roles first if you have no work experience — many senior data scientists in Pakistan started as analysts.

A degree in computer science, statistics or a related field helps with larger employers, but is not a hard requirement for most software houses and startups if the portfolio is strong.

Future Outlook for Data Scientists in Pakistan

Demand for data scientists is growing as banks, telecoms and e-commerce companies invest more in AI, but the bar is also rising: employers increasingly expect candidates to show a deployed project, not just coursework. Generative AI tools have made basic model-building faster, so companies now pay more for people who can frame the right business problem and validate a model properly, and less for people who can only run a standard library function on clean data.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average data scientist salary in Pakistan per month?

About PKR 150,000 to PKR 500,000 per month, though roles that are really data-analyst work in disguise pay toward the lower end.

How much does a junior data scientist earn in Pakistan?

Junior data scientists usually earn PKR 80,000 to PKR 150,000 per month, working mainly on data cleaning, SQL and basic models under supervision.

What is the difference between a data analyst and a data scientist in pay?

Data analysts typically earn PKR 90,000 to 250,000, while data scientists earn PKR 150,000 to 500,000, because data scientists build and validate models rather than only reporting on existing data.

Which programming languages matter most for data scientists?

Python and SQL are essential for almost every role. R is used in some research or academic settings.

Can data scientists work remotely from Pakistan?

Yes. Many work full-time or on contract for US, UK and Gulf companies, usually paid in dollars, often in the PKR 400,000 to 1,200,000+ equivalent range.

Which city has the best data science opportunities in Pakistan?

Karachi generally pays the most because of its concentration of banks and fintechs, followed closely by Lahore and Islamabad.

Is data science a good career in Pakistan?

Yes, but the title is used loosely by employers, so check whether a specific job is really model-building work or analyst work before comparing salaries.

Do I need a degree to become a data scientist?

It helps with larger employers, but many software houses and startups hire based on a strong portfolio of real projects and interview performance.

Can software engineers switch to data science?

Yes. Software engineers already have the coding skills; the gap to close is usually statistics, ML theory and experiment design.

Which industry pays data scientists the most in Pakistan?

Banking and fintech, because models like fraud detection and credit scoring have a direct, measurable financial impact.

Conclusion

A data scientist in Pakistan typically earns PKR 150,000 to PKR 500,000 per month, but the title covers a wide range of actual jobs, from analyst-style reporting to full model deployment. Before comparing offers, check what the role actually involves day to day. Strengthening Python, SQL, statistics and a real project portfolio does more for pay than certificates, and remote roles for international employers remain one of the fastest ways to increase income once you have 2-3 years of experience.
