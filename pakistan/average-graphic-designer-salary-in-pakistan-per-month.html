Graphic Designer Salary in Pakistan: Quick Answer

A Graphic Designer in Pakistan earns PKR 40,000 to PKR 220,000 per month in full-time employed roles. That number alone understates the field, though — unlike most careers on this site, a large share of Pakistani graphic designers' real income comes from freelance work, where pricing is set per project rather than per month and experienced designers can out-earn their salaried peers.

Freelance Pricing: Where the Real Money Is

Graphic design is unusually well-suited to freelancing because clients can see finished work before paying and don't need the designer physically present. Many experienced Pakistani designers work full-time employed and freelance on the side, or go fully freelance once they have a strong portfolio. Typical project rates vary enormously with client location — local Pakistani clients pay much less than international ones for the same work.

Project typeLocal client price (PKR)International client price (PKR equivalent)
Logo design5,000 – 30,00025,000 – 150,000+
Social media post set (per month)8,000 – 30,00030,000 – 120,000

Monthly retainer (ongoing client)20,000 – 80,00080,000 – 350,000+

The gap between local and international client pricing is the single biggest lever a Pakistani graphic designer has over their income — bigger than years of experience alone. Designers who build a portfolio strong enough to attract international clients (through Behance, Dribbble, Upwork or direct outreach) can multiply their income several times over compared to serving only local clients, without needing a different skill set, just a different client base.

Moving into UI/UX Design

The clearest upward path from graphic design is UI/UX design, which typically pays PKR 80,000 to PKR 300,000+ per month — noticeably above general graphic design at the same experience level, because it requires understanding user behaviour and interaction design on top of visual skill, not just visual skill alone. Designers who add Figma proficiency, basic usability principles and an understanding of how designs get built in actual products are well positioned to move into this higher-paying track after 2-3 years of graphic design experience.

For graphic design specifically, the freelance route (above) matters more than relocation. An in-house job abroad usually needs a work visa and, for most Western countries, a degree-equivalence process — a slower, harder path than for coding-based careers on this site. Freelancing for the same international clients while living in Pakistan sidesteps all of that, which is why experienced designers more often grow income by building an international freelance client base than by trying to relocate for an employed role.

How to Become a Graphic Designer in Pakistan

  1. Learn the core tools properly: Photoshop and Illustrator first, InDesign if print work interests you.
  2. Build a focused portfolio of 8-10 strong pieces rather than many weak ones — quality is judged by the best work shown, not the quantity.
  3. Publish the portfolio on Behance or Dribbble, since this is where both local employers and international freelance clients look.
  4. Take on a few local or small freelance projects early to build real client experience and testimonials.
  5. Once the portfolio is strong, target international freelance clients (Upwork, direct outreach, referrals) where the rate gap described above makes the biggest difference to income.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average Graphic Designer salary in Pakistan per month?

About PKR 40,000 to PKR 220,000 per month for full-time employed roles. Active freelancers serving international clients often earn more.

How much does a fresh graphic designer earn in Pakistan?

Fresh graduates typically earn PKR 35,000 to PKR 60,000 per month, mostly on social media graphics and basic layouts under supervision.

Can graphic designers earn more freelancing than in a full-time job?

Yes, often. International clients typically pay several times more per project than local clients, so designers with a strong portfolio and international clients can out-earn an equivalent salaried role.

How much does a logo design cost in Pakistan?

Local clients typically pay PKR 5,000 to PKR 30,000; international clients commonly pay the equivalent of PKR 25,000 to 150,000+ for the same scope of work.

Which software skills matter most for graphic designers?

Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator are the baseline. InDesign matters for print work, Figma is increasingly expected, and After Effects (motion graphics) commands higher pay.

Should graphic designers learn UI/UX design?

It's a strong upward path. UI/UX designers typically earn PKR 80,000 to PKR 300,000+ per month, noticeably more than general graphic design at the same experience level.

Does city matter for a graphic designer's salary in Pakistan?

Less than for most careers, since freelance clients don't care about location — this is one of the more location-independent careers on this site.

Is graphic design a good career in Pakistan?

Yes, particularly for designers who build a portfolio strong enough to attract international clients, since that's where the real income gap lies, more than years of experience alone.

Conclusion

A Graphic Designer in Pakistan typically earns PKR 40,000 to PKR 220,000 per month in full-time roles, but the real story is freelancing: because international clients pay several times more than local ones for the same work, a strong portfolio and an international client base matter more to lifetime income than years of experience alone. Moving toward UI/UX design is the clearest path to higher structured salaries, while building freelance clients abroad is the clearest path to higher income without relocating.
