Average Cardiologist Salary in Pakistan

Cardiology is one of the most prestigious and highest-paying medical specialties in Pakistan. Cardiologists diagnose, treat and manage diseases affecting the heart and cardiovascular system, including coronary artery disease, heart failure, hypertension, arrhythmias and congenital heart conditions. Because cardiovascular disease remains one of the leading causes of illness worldwide, qualified Cardiologists continue to enjoy strong demand across public hospitals, private cardiac centres and international healthcare organisations.

The average Cardiologist salary in Pakistan per month varies according to qualifications, FCPS specialization, years of clinical experience, employer type, hospital reputation, city and private consultation practice. Newly qualified specialists usually begin with consultant-level salaries, while experienced Heart Specialists working in leading hospitals often earn significantly higher incomes through surgeries, diagnostic procedures and private patient consultations.

House Officer Salary

After completing MBBS, doctors begin their professional journey as House Officers. During this one-year internship they rotate through various hospital departments, including Cardiology, Internal Medicine, Emergency Medicine and Surgery. Although House Officers are not yet Cardiologists, this stage provides essential clinical exposure that prepares doctors for postgraduate specialization.

House Officers working in recognised teaching hospitals generally earn between PKR 70,000 and PKR 120,000 per month. Salaries vary depending on the province, hospital funding and government pay scales.

Medical Officer Salary

Doctors who work as Medical Officers gain valuable experience managing patients independently before entering specialist residency programmes. Many Medical Officers work in emergency departments, cardiac units, intensive care units and general medicine wards where they strengthen their diagnostic and patient management skills.

Medical Officers usually earn between PKR 100,000 and PKR 200,000 per month. Doctors employed by private hospitals or specialised cardiac centres may receive additional allowances depending on workload and shift responsibilities.

FCPS Cardiology Resident Salary

After securing admission into FCPS Cardiology or another recognised postgraduate training programme, doctors begin specialist residency. Residents work closely with senior Cardiologists while learning advanced cardiac investigations, echocardiography, coronary care, catheterisation procedures and intensive cardiac patient management.

An FCPS Cardiology Resident generally earns between PKR 120,000 and PKR 250,000 per month. Residency programmes also provide invaluable clinical experience that forms the foundation of a successful consultant career.

Consultant Cardiologist Salary

Consultants independently diagnose complex cardiovascular diseases, supervise junior doctors, perform specialised cardiac procedures and lead multidisciplinary cardiac care teams.

Consultant Cardiologists commonly earn between PKR 400,000 and PKR 900,000 per month. Specialists working in renowned cardiac hospitals, private healthcare groups and tertiary care centres often receive higher compensation packages together with consultation income.

Senior Consultant Cardiologist Salary

Experienced Senior Consultants frequently earn between PKR 900,000 and PKR 1,800,000+ per month. Those with successful private practices, international fellowships or leadership roles may generate substantially higher overall monthly earnings.

BS Cardiology Salary in Pakistan Per Month

One of the most common misconceptions among students concerns the BS Cardiology salary in Pakistan per month. A BS Cardiology degree is an allied health sciences qualification and does not qualify a graduate to work as a Consultant Cardiologist. Instead, graduates usually work as Cardiac Technologists, Cardiology Technologists or Cardiac Catheterization Laboratory professionals supporting Cardiologists during diagnostic and interventional procedures.

Professionals holding a BS Cardiology degree generally earn between PKR 45,000 and PKR 120,000 per month, depending on experience, employer and technical skills. Experienced technologists working in specialised cardiac hospitals may receive higher salaries and additional allowances.

ℹ️
Important Career Difference

A Consultant Cardiologist must first complete MBBS followed by postgraduate specialist training such as FCPS Cardiology or an equivalent qualification. A BS Cardiology graduate works in an allied healthcare role and follows a different career pathway focused on cardiac diagnostic technology and patient support services.

Electrocardiography remains one of the most commonly performed cardiac investigations worldwide. ECG records the electrical activity of the heart and helps diagnose arrhythmias, myocardial infarction, conduction abnormalities and numerous cardiovascular conditions. Every Cardiologist must possess strong ECG interpretation skills because accurate diagnosis directly influences patient management.

Although ECG is considered a basic investigation, expert interpretation remains an essential professional skill throughout a Cardiologist's career.

Cardiac CT Scan

Cardiologists trained in Cardiac CT interpretation remain highly valuable as more hospitals introduce advanced imaging facilities.

Cardiac MRI

Cardiac Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) provides highly detailed images of heart muscles, valves, blood vessels and surrounding tissues. It plays an important role in diagnosing cardiomyopathy, congenital heart disease, myocarditis and complex cardiovascular abnormalities.

Advanced MRI expertise significantly strengthens a Cardiologist's diagnostic capability and professional reputation.

IVUS (Intravascular Ultrasound)

IVUS allows Cardiologists to visualise coronary arteries from inside the blood vessel during interventional procedures. It helps determine plaque characteristics, vessel size and optimal stent placement, leading to safer and more accurate coronary interventions.

OCT (Optical Coherence Tomography)

Optical Coherence Tomography provides extremely high-resolution images of coronary arteries. This technology enables Interventional Cardiologists to assess plaque morphology, optimise stent deployment and evaluate procedural outcomes with remarkable precision.

Fractional Flow Reserve (FFR)

Fractional Flow Reserve measures blood flow across narrowed coronary arteries and helps determine whether a blockage requires angioplasty or can be managed medically. FFR reduces unnecessary interventions while improving treatment decisions.

How to Become a Cardiologist in Pakistan (Step-by-Step Career Roadmap)

One of the most frequently searched questions by students is "How to become a Cardiologist in Pakistan?" Becoming a Cardiologist is a long but highly rewarding journey that requires dedication, continuous learning and years of clinical training. From completing FSc Pre-Medical to becoming a Consultant Cardiologist, the process typically takes more than a decade of education and practical experience.

The roadmap below explains each stage of the journey, including academic qualifications, licensing requirements, postgraduate specialization and career opportunities available after becoming a Consultant Cardiologist.

Step 1 – Complete FSc Pre-Medical

The journey begins after Matriculation by enrolling in FSc Pre-Medical. Students study Biology, Chemistry and Physics while building the academic foundation required for medical college admission. Strong grades significantly improve the chances of securing admission into reputable medical universities.

Students interested in Cardiology should focus on developing analytical thinking, scientific understanding and disciplined study habits from this stage onward.

Step 2 – Pass MDCAT

The Medical and Dental College Admission Test (MDCAT) is the entrance examination required for admission into recognised medical colleges in Pakistan. A competitive MDCAT score, combined with strong academic performance, increases the likelihood of admission into leading MBBS programmes.

Preparation usually includes intensive revision of Biology, Chemistry, Physics and logical reasoning together with regular practice tests.

🎯
Student Tip

Students aiming for Cardiology should target admission into recognised medical colleges with strong teaching hospitals because clinical exposure during MBBS plays an important role in future specialization.

Step 3 – Complete MBBS

The MBBS programme normally takes five years and covers anatomy, physiology, pathology, pharmacology, medicine, surgery, paediatrics, obstetrics, cardiology rotations and many other clinical subjects.

During the later years of MBBS, students gain practical experience through hospital rotations, patient examinations and supervised clinical training. This period helps future Cardiologists develop communication skills, diagnostic thinking and professional ethics.

💰
Salary Note

MBBS students do not receive Consultant-level salaries. Professional income begins after graduation and generally increases substantially after postgraduate specialization in Cardiology.

Step 4 – Complete House Job

Following MBBS graduation, every doctor completes a one-year House Job in recognised hospitals. During this period, doctors rotate through different departments including Internal Medicine, Emergency Medicine, Surgery and Cardiology while treating patients under consultant supervision.

The House Job develops clinical confidence, decision-making ability and practical medical skills before independent medical practice.

Step 5 – Obtain PMDC Registration

After successfully completing the House Job, doctors become eligible for full professional registration with the Pakistan Medical and Dental Council (PMDC). Registration is essential for practising medicine legally and applying for postgraduate residency programmes.

Step 6 – Enter FCPS or MD Cardiology Training

Doctors who wish to become Cardiologists must complete recognised postgraduate specialist training. Most doctors pursue FCPS Cardiology, while others may follow equivalent specialist pathways depending on institutional requirements.

Residency training includes advanced education in cardiovascular medicine, coronary artery disease, hypertension, echocardiography, cardiac catheterization, intensive cardiac care, angiography and emergency cardiac interventions.

Training usually lasts between four and five years before doctors become eligible for Consultant Cardiologist positions.

📌
Salary Note

Residents receive training salaries that are generally lower than Consultant Cardiologist salaries. Significant salary growth usually occurs after completing specialist qualifications.

Step 7 – Become a Consultant Cardiologist

After completing postgraduate specialist training and meeting all professional requirements, doctors become Consultant Cardiologists. Consultants independently diagnose cardiovascular diseases, supervise junior doctors, perform specialised cardiac procedures and manage complex heart patients.

Many Consultants later establish private clinics, pursue fellowships abroad or specialise further in Interventional Cardiology, Electrophysiology, Cardiac Imaging or Heart Failure management.

Step 8 – Continue Professional Development

Learning never stops in Cardiology. Successful specialists regularly attend international conferences, complete fellowships, publish research papers, participate in clinical trials and learn emerging technologies such as AI-assisted diagnosis, structural heart interventions and advanced cardiac imaging.

Continuous education not only improves patient care but also strengthens professional reputation and long-term earning potential.

To become a Cardiologist in Pakistan, students complete FSc Pre-Medical, pass MDCAT, complete MBBS, finish House Job and then pursue postgraduate specialization such as FCPS Cardiology, MD Cardiology or equivalent specialist training.
