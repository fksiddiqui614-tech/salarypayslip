How to Become a Dermatologist in Pakistan

Becoming a Dermatologist in Pakistan requires completing medical education, clinical training and postgraduate specialization in Dermatology. The journey usually takes several years but offers excellent career growth, stable employment and strong private practice opportunities.

1

Complete FSc (Pre-Medical)

Study Biology, Chemistry and Physics while preparing for MDCAT to qualify for admission into an accredited medical college.

2

Earn an MBBS Degree

Complete the five-year MBBS programme from a PMDC-recognised medical university to build a strong medical foundation.

3

Complete House Job

Finish the compulsory one-year House Job while gaining practical experience in different clinical departments.

4

Complete FCPS or MD Dermatology

Specialise in Dermatology through FCPS Dermatology or another recognised postgraduate programme to become a qualified Skin Specialist.

5

Start Professional Practice

Work in government hospitals, private hospitals, skin clinics or establish a private dermatology clinic while continuing professional development.
