Gynecologist Salary in Pakistan Overview

Gynecology is one of the most important medical specialties in Pakistan, focusing on women's reproductive health, pregnancy care, childbirth, hormonal disorders, infertility management and complex surgical procedures. Gynecologists play a vital role in improving maternal healthcare outcomes and providing specialised medical services throughout different stages of a woman's life.

The demand for qualified Gynecologists continues to increase because Pakistan has a growing need for specialised women's healthcare services. Hospitals, maternity centres, private clinics and healthcare organisations require skilled specialists who can provide both medical and surgical care.

The average Gynecologist salary in Pakistan depends on several factors including MBBS qualification, FCPS or postgraduate specialization, years of experience, hospital type, city, surgical expertise, reputation and private consultation practice.

Early-career doctors usually start their professional journey through House Job, Medical Officer positions and postgraduate training. After completing specialist qualifications, Gynecologists can progress towards Consultant roles, senior hospital positions and independent private practices.

How to Become a Gynecologist in Pakistan

Becoming a Gynecologist in Pakistan requires several years of medical education, clinical training and postgraduate specialization. The journey starts with medical college admission and continues through MBBS, House Job, residency training and specialist qualification.

Gynecology is considered a highly specialised medical career because doctors must develop expertise in women's health, pregnancy management, reproductive medicine, surgical procedures and emergency healthcare.

Step 1: Complete FSc Pre-Medical

Students interested in becoming Gynecologists usually begin by completing FSc Pre-Medical with subjects including Biology, Chemistry and Physics. Strong academic performance is important for admission into medical universities.

Step 2: Pass MDCAT Examination

After FSc, students must qualify for the Medical and Dental College Admission Test (MDCAT) to apply for MBBS programs in recognised medical institutions.

Step 3: Complete MBBS Degree

The MBBS degree normally takes five years and provides foundational knowledge in anatomy, physiology, pathology, medicine, surgery and clinical practice.

Step 4: Complete House Job

After MBBS, doctors complete a one-year House Job to gain practical experience in different medical departments including medicine, surgery and obstetrics & gynecology.

Step 5: Postgraduate Specialization

Doctors pursue postgraduate training such as FCPS Obstetrics & Gynecology, MS Gynecology or equivalent specialist programs to become qualified Gynecologists.

Step 6: Become Consultant Gynecologist

After completing specialist training and gaining clinical experience, doctors can work as Consultant Gynecologists in hospitals, maternity centres and private practices.

🎓
Career Timeline Note

The complete journey from FSc Pre-Medical to Consultant Gynecologist usually requires more than 10 years of education and medical training. Continuous learning is essential because healthcare practices and technologies continue to evolve.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average Gynecologist salary in Pakistan per month?

The average Gynecologist salary in Pakistan ranges from approximately PKR 300,000 to PKR 1,200,000 per month. Senior consultants and private practice specialists may earn significantly higher depending on experience, reputation, city and patient demand.

What is the private Gynecologist salary in Pakistan per month?

Private Gynecologists may earn between PKR 500,000 and PKR 2,000,000+ per month depending on hospital affiliation, consultation fees, surgical procedures, clinic growth and professional reputation.

How to become a Gynecologist in Pakistan?

To become a Gynecologist, students complete FSc Pre-Medical, pass MDCAT, earn an MBBS degree, complete House Job and then pursue postgraduate specialization such as FCPS Obstetrics & Gynecology or MS Gynecology.

Is Gynecology a good career in Pakistan?

Yes. Gynecology is considered one of the strong medical career options in Pakistan because women's healthcare demand continues to grow and specialist doctors have opportunities in hospitals, clinics and international markets.

What is the MBBS Doctor salary in Pakistan?

MBBS doctors in Pakistan generally earn between PKR 60,000 and PKR 200,000 per month depending on experience, employer type and location. Specialist doctors usually earn significantly higher after postgraduate training.

Which Gynecology specialization has the highest salary?

Infertility specialists, maternal fetal medicine specialists and gynecological surgeons generally have higher earning potential because they provide advanced specialised healthcare services.

Which city offers the highest Gynecologist salary in Pakistan?

Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad generally offer higher salaries because they have larger healthcare markets, advanced hospitals, specialist centres and greater patient demand.

What is the future scope of Gynecology in Pakistan?

The future scope of Gynecology remains promising due to increasing healthcare awareness, reproductive medicine growth, fertility services expansion and adoption of modern medical technology.
