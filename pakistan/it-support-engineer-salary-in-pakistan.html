IT Support Engineer Salary in Pakistan Overview

IT Support Engineers are responsible for maintaining computers, operating systems, business software, networks and workplace technology that enable organizations to operate efficiently. Their daily responsibilities include troubleshooting technical issues, installing hardware and software, managing user accounts, resolving connectivity problems and providing technical assistance to employees across different departments.

The average IT Support Engineer salary in Pakistan depends on professional experience, technical skills, certifications, employer type, industry, city and knowledge of enterprise technologies such as Windows Server, Microsoft 365, Active Directory, networking, virtualization and cloud platforms. Professionals who continuously develop their technical expertise often progress into systems administration, infrastructure engineering and cloud support roles with higher earning potential.

💻

Most IT Support Engineers in Pakistan earn between PKR 90,000 and PKR 350,000 per month, while senior infrastructure engineers, systems administrators and enterprise IT specialists may earn considerably higher salaries depending on responsibilities and technical expertise.

How to Become an IT Support Engineer in Pakistan

An IT Support Engineer is responsible for maintaining computers, servers, networks, cloud services and business applications that keep organizations operating efficiently. The profession offers excellent long-term career opportunities because every industry depends on reliable information technology infrastructure. Individuals who continuously improve their technical knowledge and gain practical experience can progress into higher-paying infrastructure, cloud and cybersecurity roles.

Most professionals begin with a computing qualification or technical diploma before developing practical troubleshooting skills through internships, entry-level jobs and professional certifications. As experience increases, engineers gradually move into systems administration, cloud computing, enterprise infrastructure and IT management positions.

1

Complete Your Education

Start by completing Intermediate (ICS, FSc or FA) followed by a BS in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering or a related computing field. Diploma holders can also begin their careers through technical institutes and vocational training programs.

2

Develop Technical Skills

Learn Windows installation, computer hardware, networking fundamentals, Active Directory, Microsoft 365, operating systems, troubleshooting methodologies and basic server administration. Practical lab experience is extremely valuable during this stage.

3

Earn Professional Certifications

Industry certifications such as CompTIA A+, Microsoft Certified, Cisco CCNA, Microsoft Azure or AWS Cloud Practitioner improve technical credibility and increase opportunities for enterprise IT support positions.

4

Gain Practical Experience

Work as a Help Desk Technician, Desktop Support Engineer or Junior IT Support Engineer to build hands-on experience with enterprise environments, networking, servers and cloud technologies.

5

Specialize & Continue Learning

Choose a long-term specialization such as Systems Administration, Cloud Engineering, Cybersecurity, Networking or DevOps. Continuous learning and practical experience are the keys to long-term salary growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Below are some of the most common questions people ask about IT Support Engineer salaries, career growth, qualifications, certifications and job opportunities in Pakistan.

What is the average IT Support Engineer salary in Pakistan per month?

The average IT Support Engineer salary in Pakistan generally ranges from PKR 90,000 to PKR 250,000 per month. Entry-level professionals usually earn lower salaries, while experienced infrastructure specialists, cloud engineers and senior IT professionals can earn significantly more depending on technical expertise and employer.

How much does a fresh IT Support Engineer earn in Pakistan?

Fresh graduates and entry-level IT Support Engineers typically earn between PKR 55,000 and PKR 90,000 per month. Salaries depend on educational background, internships, certifications, technical skills and employer requirements.

Which city offers the highest IT Support Engineer salaries?

Karachi, Islamabad and Lahore generally offer the highest salaries because these cities have the largest concentration of software companies, multinational organizations, banks, telecom operators and enterprise businesses.

Do certifications increase IT Support Engineer salaries?

Yes. Professional certifications such as CompTIA A+, Microsoft Certifications, Cisco CCNA, Microsoft Azure and AWS Cloud Practitioner improve technical credibility and often lead to better salary packages and faster promotions.

Which industries pay the highest salaries?

Software companies, multinational organizations, banking institutions, telecom operators and international remote employers usually provide the highest salaries because they rely heavily on enterprise infrastructure and cloud technologies.

Can IT Support Engineers work remotely?

Yes. Many experienced IT Support Engineers now work remotely for international companies, providing enterprise technical support, Microsoft 365 administration, cloud infrastructure management and help desk services from Pakistan.

What qualifications are required to become an IT Support Engineer?

Most employers prefer candidates with a BS in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering or related disciplines. However, many successful professionals also build careers through diploma programs, technical institutes and industry certifications.

Which technical skills are most valuable?

Microsoft 365, Windows Server, Active Directory, cloud computing, networking, virtualization, cybersecurity, PowerShell scripting and enterprise troubleshooting are among the highest-paying IT support skills.

Can IT Support Engineers become Cloud Engineers?

Yes. Many IT Support Engineers progress into Cloud Engineering after gaining experience with Microsoft Azure, AWS, virtualization, Windows Server and enterprise infrastructure administration.

Is IT Support Engineering a good career in Pakistan?

Yes. IT Support Engineering offers stable employment, continuous learning opportunities and strong long-term career growth. Professionals who specialize in cloud computing, cybersecurity, systems administration and automation often achieve significantly higher salaries.

How long does it take to become a Senior IT Support Engineer?

Most professionals require approximately 5–8 years of practical enterprise experience together with certifications and continuous technical learning before progressing into senior infrastructure or systems administration roles.

What is the future scope of IT Support Engineering?

The future remains very promising as organizations continue investing in cloud computing, cybersecurity, virtualization, automation and enterprise digital transformation. Demand for experienced IT Support Engineers is expected to remain strong across Pakistan and international markets.
