IT Support Engineer Salary in Pakistan: Quick Answer

An IT Support Engineer in Pakistan earns PKR 55,000 to PKR 350,000 per month in most roles, with infrastructure specialists going higher. Unlike a licensed profession, there's no single credential that unlocks the top of this range — pay tracks a specific, checkable stack of skills (Windows Server, Active Directory, one cloud platform, networking) more than years of experience alone. Two engineers with the same "IT Support Engineer" title and the same years of experience can be paid very differently depending on which of these they actually know.

Why Certifications Matter More Than Degree Here

Unlike a doctor or an accountant, an IT Support Engineer's job title doesn't map to a single fixed body of knowledge — "support" can mean anything from resetting passwords to running an organisation's entire Azure tenant. Because of that spread, employers lean on specific, checkable certifications (Microsoft, Cisco, AWS) as a fast way to filter for what someone can actually do, more than they lean on a computer science degree, which tells them less about hands-on enterprise skills.

This is also why the field rewards specialising in one enterprise technology stack over spreading thin across many. An engineer who is genuinely strong in Microsoft 365 and Windows Server administration, and can speak in detail about real incidents they've resolved, is generally worth more to an employer than one who has surface familiarity with five different platforms but depth in none.

Unlike a licensed profession, moving abroad in this field mostly comes down to certifications and a work visa, not a separate national licensing exam — Microsoft, Cisco and AWS certifications carry the same weight in Dubai, London or Toronto as they do in Karachi. This makes IT support a comparatively easier field to relocate in than, say, medicine, but it also means the local job market has more competition for the same visible credentials, so relocation typically needs a specific employer sponsor rather than just the certificate alone. The Gulf is the most common first move for Pakistani IT support engineers because of geographic proximity, no language barrier for most roles, and lower relocation cost than the UK, Canada or US.

How to Start a Career in IT Support in Pakistan

  1. Get a computing background — a BS in CS/IT is common, but a diploma (DIT/DAE) plus real lab practice works too, since certifications matter more than the degree title.
  2. Earn CompTIA A+ or equivalent fundamentals and pair it with real troubleshooting, not just exam prep.
  3. Get hands-on with Windows Server and Active Directory in a home lab or internship — this is the single most requested skill in local job ads.
  4. Add one cloud certification (Azure Fundamentals or AWS Cloud Practitioner) once comfortable with on-prem infrastructure.
  5. Apply for Help Desk or Desktop Support roles first, and treat the first 1-2 years as building toward Systems Administration or Cloud Support rather than staying in pure ticket-resolution work.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average IT Support Engineer salary in Pakistan per month?

About PKR 55,000 to PKR 350,000 per month, depending mainly on which enterprise technologies (Microsoft, cloud, networking) the engineer can actually support.

How much does a fresh IT Support Engineer earn in Pakistan?

Fresh graduates typically earn PKR 55,000 to PKR 90,000 per month, working mainly on desktop support and end-user tickets.

Do certifications increase IT Support Engineer salaries?

Yes, more than most other factors on this page. Microsoft, Cisco and cloud certifications, backed by real hands-on experience, are the clearest way to move up the pay range.

Which city offers the highest IT Support Engineer salaries?

Islamabad and Karachi generally pay the most, followed by Lahore, due to the concentration of multinational offices, banks and large software companies.

Can IT Support Engineers work remotely for international companies?

Yes. Many work remote help desk or cloud support roles for overseas employers, usually earning PKR 250,000 to 700,000+ per month equivalent.

What qualifications are required to become an IT Support Engineer?

A BS in Computer Science or IT helps, but many engineers enter through diplomas (DIT/DAE) plus certifications like CompTIA A+, since practical skills matter more than the degree title.

Can IT Support Engineers move into Cloud Engineering?

Yes. It's a common path after gaining Windows Server and Active Directory experience, then adding an Azure or AWS certification.

Do Pakistani IT certifications transfer to jobs abroad?

Yes. Microsoft, Cisco and AWS certifications are recognised the same way internationally, which makes this field easier to relocate in than licensed professions, though a visa-sponsoring employer is still needed.

Is IT Support Engineering a good career in Pakistan?

Yes, especially for those who use it as a stepping stone into Systems Administration, Cloud Engineering or Cybersecurity rather than staying in entry-level ticket support long-term.

Conclusion

An IT Support Engineer in Pakistan typically earns PKR 55,000 to PKR 350,000 per month, with infrastructure specialists and remote international roles going higher. The fastest way to move up this range is stacking a specific, verifiable skill set — Microsoft infrastructure, then one cloud platform, then networking or security — over simply accumulating years in a generalist help desk role.
