Oncologist Salary by Experience

Professional experience is one of the biggest factors affecting an Oncologist's income. As doctors progress from residency to consultant practice, they gain expertise in cancer diagnosis, chemotherapy planning, multidisciplinary care and advanced treatment techniques, resulting in higher salaries.

Experience Level Estimated Monthly Salary Career Stage
Entry Level (0–2 Years) PKR 180,000 – 320,000 Junior specialist managing supervised oncology care.
Early Career (3–5 Years) PKR 320,000 – 550,000 Independent consultant with growing clinical responsibilities.
Mid-Level (6–10 Years) PKR 550,000 – 900,000 Experienced cancer specialist handling complex treatment plans.
Senior Consultant (10–15 Years) PKR 900,000 – 1,500,000+ Leads oncology teams and advanced cancer management.

How to Become an Oncologist in Pakistan

Building a successful Oncology career requires years of structured medical education, specialist training and clinical experience. The journey prepares doctors to diagnose, treat and manage different types of cancer using evidence-based therapies.

Complete FSc Pre-Medical

Study Biology, Chemistry and Physics to become eligible for medical college admission.

Earn an MBBS Degree

Complete a recognised five-year MBBS programme approved by PMDC.

Complete House Job

Gain practical clinical experience through compulsory supervised rotations.

Specialize in Oncology

Complete FCPS Oncology residency with training in cancer diagnosis, chemotherapy and multidisciplinary patient care.

Become a Consultant Oncologist

Begin consultant practice, pursue fellowships and continue professional development through research and advanced cancer therapies.

🩺
Career Roadmap Insight

Successful Oncologists combine strong clinical knowledge, compassionate patient care, research involvement and continuous learning to provide effective cancer treatment throughout their careers.

Oncology Specializations and Career Paths

Oncology is a broad medical specialty with multiple subspecialties focused on different cancer types and treatment methods. Choosing a specialization can influence career direction, research opportunities and earning potential.

Medical Oncology

Focuses on chemotherapy, immunotherapy, targeted therapy and long-term cancer management.

Radiation Oncology

Uses advanced radiation technology to destroy cancer cells while protecting healthy tissue.

Surgical Oncology

Specializes in removing tumors and performing complex cancer-related surgical procedures.

Pediatric Oncology

Diagnoses and treats childhood cancers using specialized treatment protocols.

Hematologic Oncology

Manages blood cancers including leukemia, lymphoma and multiple myeloma.

Gynecologic Oncology

Treats cancers affecting the female reproductive system through surgery and multidisciplinary care.
