Common Oral & Maxillofacial Surgical Procedures

Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons manage conditions affecting the mouth, jaws, facial bones and surrounding soft tissues. Their work extends beyond routine dental treatment and often involves complex surgical intervention.

🦷 Impacted Wisdom Tooth Surgery

Removal of impacted third molars is one of the most frequently performed procedures, helping prevent infection, pain and damage to nearby teeth.

🦴 Corrective Jaw Surgery

Orthognathic surgery improves jaw alignment, chewing function, facial balance and certain speech-related problems.

🚑 Facial Trauma Reconstruction

Emergency treatment for fractures involving the jaw, cheekbones and facial skeleton following accidents or sports injuries.

🧬 Oral Tumour & Cyst Surgery

Diagnosis and surgical management of benign and malignant lesions affecting the oral cavity and surrounding facial structures.

🦿 Bone Grafting Procedures

Bone augmentation techniques help restore lost jawbone before implant placement or reconstructive surgery.

🪥 Advanced Dental Implant Surgery

Placement of dental implants for patients requiring permanent tooth replacement and functional oral rehabilitation.

Frequently Asked Questions About Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon Salary in Pakistan

How much does an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon earn in Pakistan?

Most Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons earn between PKR 180,000 and PKR 700,000+ per month. Income depends on surgical experience, consultant status, hospital appointments, private practice and specialised procedures.

What qualifications are required to become an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon?

A recognised BDS degree followed by postgraduate specialist training such as FCPS Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery is typically required before working as an independent consultant.

What types of surgery does an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon perform?

These specialists perform facial trauma surgery, wisdom tooth removal, corrective jaw surgery, oral tumour treatment, bone grafting, dental implant surgery and other complex oral surgical procedures.

Is Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery a high-paying dental specialty?

Yes. It is considered one of the highest-paying dental specialties because of its advanced surgical training, hospital-based responsibilities and highly specialised clinical services.

Which hospitals employ Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons in Pakistan?

They commonly work in teaching hospitals, tertiary care medical centres, military hospitals, cancer hospitals and specialist private healthcare institutions.

Can an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon establish a private practice?

Yes. Many experienced consultants operate successful private surgical clinics or work with advanced dental hospitals, significantly increasing their overall income.

Which surgical skills have the greatest impact on salary?

Expertise in facial trauma reconstruction, orthognathic surgery, implant surgery, oral pathology management and craniofacial reconstruction can improve earning potential.

Which city offers the best career opportunities for Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons?

Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad generally provide the largest number of consultant positions because they have advanced hospitals, medical universities and specialist surgical centres.

Do Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons treat accident and trauma patients?

Yes. They frequently manage facial fractures, jaw injuries, soft tissue trauma and emergency maxillofacial conditions in hospital emergency departments.

What is the difference between an Oral Surgeon and an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon?

An Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon receives advanced specialist training to manage diseases, injuries and deformities affecting both the oral cavity and facial structures, including the jaws and facial bones.

Can Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons perform dental implant surgery?

Yes. They are highly trained to perform implant placement, bone grafting and surgical procedures required for complex implant rehabilitation.

Are Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons in demand internationally?

Yes. Countries with advanced healthcare systems continue to recruit qualified surgeons who meet local licensing, registration and specialist certification requirements.

How many years does it take to become an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon?

The journey usually includes a BDS degree, house job, postgraduate residency and several years of supervised surgical training before becoming a consultant.

What is the future scope of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery in Pakistan?

Demand is expected to grow because of increasing facial trauma cases, dental implant procedures, reconstructive surgery, oral cancer treatment and modern hospital expansion.

Can Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons become university professors?

Yes. Experienced consultants with teaching experience, research publications and academic qualifications can pursue careers in medical and dental universities.

Conclusion

Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery is one of the most advanced specialties within dentistry, combining surgical precision with comprehensive patient care. Specialists treat facial trauma, jaw abnormalities, oral diseases and complex reconstructive conditions while working alongside multidisciplinary healthcare teams.

Career growth depends on continuous learning, surgical excellence, clinical experience and professional reputation. Surgeons who develop expertise in trauma management, reconstructive procedures, implant surgery and hospital leadership often enjoy excellent long-term career prospects and strong earning potential in both public and private healthcare sectors.
