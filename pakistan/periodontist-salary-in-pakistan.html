Common Periodontal Disease Treatments

Periodontists diagnose and manage a wide range of gum diseases using both non-surgical and surgical procedures. The complexity of treatment often influences consultant fees and long-term earning potential.

🪥 Scaling & Root Planing

Deep cleaning procedures remove plaque and calculus below the gum line to control periodontal disease.

🦷 Gum Grafting

Soft tissue grafts help restore receding gums, improve aesthetics and protect exposed tooth roots.

🩺 Pocket Reduction Surgery

Surgical treatment reduces periodontal pockets and improves long-term gum health.

😁 Crown Lengthening

Reshaping gum tissue improves both restorative dentistry outcomes and smile appearance.

🦴 Bone Grafting

Bone regeneration procedures restore jawbone lost because of advanced periodontal disease.

🦷 Dental Implant Maintenance

Long-term implant care helps maintain healthy supporting tissues and improve implant success rates.

Dental Implant & Regenerative Procedures

Modern Periodontology extends far beyond gum disease treatment. Today's Periodontists routinely perform advanced implant surgery, bone regeneration and digital treatment planning that improve both oral health and long-term treatment success. Specialists skilled in these procedures are often among the highest-paid professionals in dentistry.

🦷 Dental Implant Placement

Dental implants replace missing teeth by surgically placing titanium implants into the jawbone, providing a durable and highly functional long-term solution.

🦴 Guided Bone Regeneration (GBR)

Bone regeneration techniques rebuild deficient jawbone before implant placement, improving stability and treatment outcomes.

🌱 Soft Tissue Regeneration

Soft tissue grafting restores healthy gum tissue around natural teeth and dental implants while improving aesthetics and periodontal health.

✨ Laser Periodontal Therapy

Laser-assisted periodontal procedures reduce discomfort, improve precision and support faster healing in selected clinical cases.

💻 Digital Implant Planning

Computer-guided implant planning improves surgical accuracy, treatment predictability and overall patient safety.

🩻 CBCT Imaging

Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) provides detailed three-dimensional imaging for implant planning and complex periodontal surgery.

🔬
Technology Insight

Periodontists who master implant dentistry, regenerative procedures and digital surgical planning generally enjoy stronger consultant careers, higher patient demand and excellent long-term earning potential.
