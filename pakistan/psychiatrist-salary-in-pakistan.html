How to Become a Psychiatrist in Pakistan

Becoming a Psychiatrist requires completing medical education followed by specialist postgraduate training in Psychiatry. The process usually takes several years of academic study, clinical training and supervised practice.

Complete FSc (Pre-Medical)

Study Biology, Chemistry and Physics while preparing for medical college admission.

Earn an MBBS Degree

Complete the five-year MBBS programme from a recognised medical university.

Complete House Job

Finish one year of compulsory clinical internship in recognised hospitals.

Complete FCPS or MD Psychiatry

Undertake postgraduate specialist training in Psychiatry with supervised clinical experience.

Become a Consultant Psychiatrist

Work in hospitals, mental healthcare centres, universities or establish private psychiatric practice.

🩺
Career Roadmap Insight

Continuous learning, research participation, communication skills and clinical experience are essential for building a successful long-term Psychiatry career.
