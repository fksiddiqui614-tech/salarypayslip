How to Become a Psychologist in Pakistan

Becoming a psychologist requires formal education, supervised practical experience and continuous professional development. The following roadmap outlines the typical pathway towards a successful psychology career.

Step 1

Complete Intermediate (FSc, FA, ICS or equivalent) from a recognised educational institution.

Step 2

Earn a BS Psychology degree from an HEC-recognised university.

Step 3

Complete an MS or MPhil in Psychology or specialise in Clinical Psychology.

Step 4

Gain supervised clinical training, internships and practical counselling experience.

Step 5

Develop expertise through professional workshops, certifications and evidence-based therapy training.

Step 6

Build your career in hospitals, private clinics, educational institutions, NGOs, corporate organisations or establish an independent private practice.

🚀
Career Roadmap Insight

Continuous learning, practical clinical experience, ethical practice and strong communication skills are essential for long-term success as a professional psychologist.
