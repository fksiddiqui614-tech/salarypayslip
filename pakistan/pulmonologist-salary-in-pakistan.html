How to Become a Pulmonologist in Pakistan

A career in Pulmonology requires years of structured education, postgraduate training and practical experience in respiratory medicine. Each stage develops the knowledge and clinical expertise needed to diagnose and manage complex lung diseases.

1

Complete FSc Pre-Medical

Build a strong academic foundation in Biology, Chemistry and Physics before applying to medical colleges.

2

Earn an MBBS Degree

Graduate from a PMDC-recognised medical university and develop core medical knowledge across all specialties.

3

Complete House Job

Gain supervised clinical experience while strengthening diagnostic, communication and patient management skills.

4

Join FCPS Pulmonology

Complete specialist residency training focused on respiratory diseases, bronchoscopy, pulmonary diagnostics and critical care.

5

Become a Consultant

Work independently as a Pulmonologist in hospitals, respiratory centres or academic institutions.

6

Pursue Fellowship & Leadership

Develop advanced expertise in sleep medicine, interventional pulmonology, intensive care or lung transplantation while expanding research and teaching responsibilities.

Career Roadmap Insight
