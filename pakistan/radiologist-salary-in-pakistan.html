How to Become a Radiologist in Pakistan

Becoming a Radiologist in Pakistan requires medical education, postgraduate specialization and professional training in diagnostic imaging. The journey usually starts with MBBS and continues through advanced Radiology training programs such as FCPS Radiology or MD Radiology.

🎓 1. Complete MBBS Degree

Students first complete FSc Pre-Medical, qualify MDCAT and earn an MBBS degree from a recognised medical university.

🏥 2. Complete House Job

After MBBS, doctors complete a one-year House Job to gain practical clinical experience before postgraduate specialization.

🩻 3. Pursue Radiology Training

Doctors can specialize through FCPS Radiology, MD Radiology or other recognised postgraduate imaging programs.

⭐ 4. Become Consultant Radiologist

After completing specialist training and gaining experience, Radiologists can work as consultants in hospitals and diagnostic centres.

Career Salary Impact
