Gross Salary vs Net Salary in Payslip

Understanding the difference between gross salary and net salary is important when creating or reviewing a salary payslip. Both figures represent different stages of the payroll calculation process.

Salary Term Meaning Example
Basic Salary The fixed amount paid to an employee before additional benefits and deductions. PKR 100,000
Gross Salary Total earnings including basic salary and additional allowances before deductions. PKR 140,000
Deductions Amounts removed from salary including taxes or other payroll adjustments. PKR 15,000
Net Salary Final amount received by the employee after deductions. PKR 125,000
📌
Salary Calculation Note

Actual salary calculations depend on employment agreements, company policies, applicable deductions and local payroll requirements.
