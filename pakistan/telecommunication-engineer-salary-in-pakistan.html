Telecommunication Engineer Salary in Pakistan: Quick Answer

A Telecommunication Engineer in Pakistan earns PKR 60,000 to PKR 600,000 per month in most roles, with 5G specialists and architects going higher. The single biggest factor isn't years of experience — it's which part of the network you actually work on. An RF engineer optimizing radio coverage, a core network engineer running the switching backbone, and a fiber transmission engineer are all "telecom engineers," but their ceilings differ by hundreds of thousands of rupees.

Vendor Certifications: Why They Matter More Than a Degree Here

Pakistan's telecom networks run almost entirely on equipment from a handful of vendors — mainly Huawei, Nokia and Ericsson. Because of this, a vendor-specific certification (Huawei HCIA/HCIP, or vendor-run training from Nokia/Ericsson) often carries more weight in hiring than a general networking certification, since it proves you can work on the exact equipment the employer already has installed.

Certification pathMonthly salary (PKR)What it signals
Cisco CCNA90,000 – 250,000General IP networking foundation
Cisco CCNP Enterprise180,000 – 400,000Advanced routing/switching for larger networks
Huawei HCIA / HCIP150,000 – 450,000Direct fit for Huawei-equipped networks, common across Pakistani operators
Vendor-specific 5G training (Huawei/Nokia/Ericsson)250,000 – 700,000+Rare skill, tied to whichever vendor's 5G gear the employer runs

The practical implication: before choosing which certification to pursue, it's worth finding out which vendor(s) your target employer actually uses. A Huawei certification is far more valuable at a Huawei-equipped operator than a generic one would be, and vice versa.

Saudi Arabia specifically has been hiring heavily for 5G rollout, fiber expansion and network operations roles tied to its national digital infrastructure projects, and Pakistani engineers with Huawei or Ericsson vendor certifications are well positioned because those are the same vendors dominant in the Saudi market. Employer sponsorship is the usual route rather than a separate national licensing exam, which makes this a comparatively accessible move for engineers who already hold a recognised vendor certification and a few years of hands-on network experience.

How to Build a Telecommunication Engineering Career in Pakistan

  1. Complete a BS in Telecommunication Engineering, Electrical Engineering or a closely related field.
  2. Build networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, routing, switching) — CCNA is a reasonable starting certification regardless of which specialization you head toward.
  3. Get field or operations experience early, ideally at a telecom operator, to see RF, transmission and core network work firsthand before specializing.
  4. Pick a specialization (RF, transmission, core network or 5G) based on what you actually did well in that first exposure, then get the matching vendor certification (Huawei/Nokia/Ericsson) for the equipment your target employers use.
  5. Once established in a specialization, 5G and cloud-telecom skills (SDN/NFV) are the clearest way to keep raising the ceiling.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average Telecommunication Engineer salary in Pakistan per month?

About PKR 60,000 to PKR 600,000 per month, with specialization (RF, core network, 5G) mattering more than years of experience alone.

How much does a fresh Telecommunication Engineer earn in Pakistan?

Fresh graduates typically earn PKR 60,000 to PKR 100,000 per month in field support or network monitoring roles.

What is the Telecommunication Engineer salary in Saudi Arabia?

Roughly SAR 84,000 to SAR 240,000 per year (about PKR 560,000 to 1,600,000 per month equivalent), commonly through employer sponsorship tied to 5G and fiber infrastructure projects.

Which telecom specialization pays the most?

5G engineering and core network engineering pay the most, up to PKR 600,000–700,000+ for senior roles, because of the scarcity of deep vendor-specific expertise.

Do Huawei or Cisco certifications matter more for telecom engineers?

It depends on the employer's equipment. Since most Pakistani operators run Huawei, Nokia or Ericsson gear, a matching vendor certification often carries more weight than a general Cisco certification for telecom-specific roles.

Which city offers the highest telecom engineering salaries in Pakistan?

Karachi and Islamabad generally pay the most, due to telecom operator headquarters, vendor offices and regulatory bodies being based there.

Can Telecommunication Engineers work remotely?

Yes, particularly in network consulting, cloud-telecom (SDN/NFV) and international project support, often paying PKR 300,000–1,000,000+ per month equivalent.

Is Telecommunication Engineering a good career in Pakistan?

Yes, especially for engineers who choose a specialization deliberately rather than staying generalist, since the pay spread between specializations is larger here than in most engineering fields.

Conclusion

A Telecommunication Engineer in Pakistan typically earns PKR 60,000 to PKR 600,000 per month, with 5G and core network specialists at the top of that range. The clearest way to raise pay here is choosing a specialization deliberately — RF, transmission, core network or 5G — and matching it with the vendor certification (Huawei, Nokia or Ericsson) relevant to your target employer's equipment, rather than accumulating years in a generalist role.
