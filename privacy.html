Overview

SalaryPayslip is an informational website that provides salary guides, career insights, and compensation-related educational content. We respect user privacy and aim to collect only minimal data required for website functionality and analytics.

We do not sell personal data or share user information with unauthorized third parties.

Important Notice

This policy applies only to data collected through the SalaryPayslip website and does not cover external websites linked from our platform.

Information We Collect

SalaryPayslip may collect limited types of information to improve website performance, user experience, and content delivery. This information is collected in a non-intrusive and general manner.

  • Basic browser and device information (such as browser type and operating system)
  • Pages visited and time spent on the website
  • General location data (non-precise, based on IP address)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies for analytics purposes

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as financial details, passwords, or private identification documents.

How We Use Information

The information collected is used for improving website performance, analyzing user behavior, and optimizing content structure.

  • To improve website content and user experience
  • To analyze traffic patterns and engagement
  • To maintain website security and prevent abuse
  • To optimize performance and loading speed

SalaryPayslip uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance user experience and collect analytical data. Cookies help us understand how users interact with our website and improve overall performance.

Users may choose to disable cookies through their browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect certain website features or functionality.
