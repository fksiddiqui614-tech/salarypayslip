What Is a Payslip in Canada?

A payslip, also called a pay stub, is an official document that shows how an employee’s salary is calculated for a specific pay period. In Canada, payslips are commonly used to explain gross income, deductions, and net pay.

Canadian employers are expected to provide clear payroll records to employees. These records are often required for tax filing, rental applications, immigration documentation, and loan approvals.

Common Information on a Canadian Payslip

  • Employee and employer details
  • Pay period and payment date
  • Gross earnings
  • CPP (Canada Pension Plan) deductions
  • EI (Employment Insurance) contributions
  • Federal and provincial income tax
  • Net pay
