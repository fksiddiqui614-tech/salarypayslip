What Is a Payslip in Schengen Countries?

A payslip, also called a salary statement, details an employee's gross earnings, deductions, and net pay. In Schengen countries (Germany, France, Spain, Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, etc.), each country has specific payroll regulations. Payslips are legally required and must show deductions like social security, health insurance, and income tax.
