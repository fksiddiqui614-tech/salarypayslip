What Is a USA Salary Slip?

A salary slip in the United States is a monthly or bi-weekly document provided by employers to employees. It clearly shows gross income, deductions, and net pay. USA payslips usually include federal income tax, state income tax (if applicable), Social Security, Medicare, and other benefit deductions.

Salary slips are commonly used for personal record keeping, employment verification, loan applications, rental agreements, and tax reference purposes.

Who Uses Salary Payslips in the USA?

  • Full-time and part-time employees
  • Freelancers and independent contractors
  • Small business owners
  • HR and payroll professionals
  • Accountants managing payroll records
