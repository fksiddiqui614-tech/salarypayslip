Anesthesiologist Salary in USA Quick Answer

Anesthesiology is one of the highest-compensated physician specialties in the United States. According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Anesthesiologists had a mean annual wage of $336,640 in May 2024. BLS reports this figure under the specific Anesthesiologist occupation, while broader physician salary measures use a separate physicians-and-surgeons classification. Actual compensation can vary considerably based on experience, practice setting, call schedules, geographic market, case volume and professional specialization.

How to Become an Anesthesiologist in the USA

Becoming an Anesthesiologist requires years of academic preparation and supervised medical training. The pathway combines medical education with specialized residency experience focused on anesthesia delivery, airway management, perioperative medicine, critical care and patient safety.

1. Complete Undergraduate Education

Students normally complete a bachelor's degree with the prerequisite science coursework required for medical school admission.

2. Earn an MD or DO

Medical school provides foundational training in anatomy, physiology, pharmacology, pathology, clinical medicine and patient care.

3. Complete Anesthesiology Residency

After medical school, physicians enter an accredited anesthesiology residency where they develop expertise in perioperative and anesthesia care.

4. Obtain Medical Licensure

Physicians must satisfy applicable examination, certification and state licensing requirements before practicing independently.

5. Pursue Fellowship Training

Additional fellowship training can prepare physicians for areas such as pain medicine, critical care, pediatric or cardiac anesthesiology.

6. Begin Independent Practice

Fully trained Anesthesiologists can pursue positions in hospitals, physician groups, academic centers, ambulatory surgery facilities and other healthcare environments.

🩺
Career Path Note

Training requirements and licensing procedures can change, and physicians should confirm current requirements with the relevant medical licensing authority, residency accreditation organizations and specialty boards before making career decisions.

Medical Schools for Future Anesthesiologists

Students interested in anesthesiology typically begin by choosing an accredited medical school that provides strong clinical exposure, supportive mentorship and opportunities to explore perioperative medicine. Medical school itself does not guarantee a residency position, so academic performance, clinical evaluations and broader professional development remain important throughout training.

Anesthesiologist Career Guide

Anesthesiology offers a physician career centered on patient safety, procedural medicine and continuous clinical decision-making. Anesthesiologists work closely with surgeons, nurses and other medical professionals while managing a patient's physiological status throughout procedures and the recovery period.

💉 Perioperative Care

Anesthesiologists evaluate patients before procedures, develop anesthesia plans and monitor them throughout surgery and recovery.

🫁 Airway Management

Advanced airway assessment and management are important components of anesthesiology, particularly during complex surgical and emergency situations.

🧠 Critical Decision-Making

Physicians must rapidly interpret vital signs and clinical changes while adjusting medications, ventilation and other aspects of patient care.

🏥 Collaborative Practice

Anesthesiologists regularly coordinate with surgeons, procedural specialists, nurses and intensive-care teams to support safe patient outcomes.

🚀
Career Development Note

Long-term professional growth can involve clinical specialization, teaching, research, medical leadership, pain management, critical care or participation in anesthesia practice management. The best pathway depends on individual interests and professional goals.

Anesthesiology Subspecialties

Anesthesiology includes several areas of advanced practice. Physicians may develop additional expertise after residency to care for specific patient groups, manage complex procedures or focus on particular aspects of perioperative and pain medicine. Subspecialty training can broaden clinical responsibilities and create different career opportunities.

❤️ Cardiac Anesthesiology

Cardiac anesthesiologists care for patients undergoing complex heart and vascular procedures and develop expertise in advanced cardiovascular monitoring and perioperative management.

👶 Pediatric Anesthesiology

Pediatric specialists provide anesthesia for infants, children and adolescents while adapting medication, airway management and monitoring approaches to younger patients.

🤰 Obstetric Anesthesiology

Obstetric anesthesiologists support patients during labor, delivery and specialized maternal procedures while considering the needs of both mother and newborn.

🧠 Pain Medicine

Pain specialists evaluate and manage acute and chronic pain using medical, interventional and multidisciplinary treatment approaches.

🚑 Critical Care Anesthesiology

Critical care specialists manage seriously ill patients in intensive-care environments and apply advanced skills in ventilation, hemodynamic support and acute medical management.

🩺 Regional Anesthesiology

Regional specialists use nerve blocks and other targeted techniques to provide anesthesia and postoperative pain control for selected surgical procedures.

💡
Subspecialty Note

Additional training may expand an Anesthesiologist's clinical scope, but compensation is not determined by subspecialty alone. Employer structure, geographic demand, procedure mix, experience and workload can have a substantial effect on total earnings.

Modern Anesthesiology Technology

Technology has changed how Anesthesiologists assess patients, deliver anesthesia and monitor physiological changes during procedures. Modern operating rooms increasingly combine clinical judgment with sophisticated monitoring systems and digital tools designed to support safer and more precise perioperative care.

📈 Advanced Patient Monitoring

Continuous monitoring of vital signs and physiological measurements helps physicians identify changes quickly and adjust treatment during anesthesia.

🔊 Ultrasound-Guided Procedures

Point-of-care ultrasound can assist with regional anesthesia, vascular access and selected bedside assessments when clinically appropriate.

💻 Anesthesia Information Systems

Digital anesthesia records help document medications, vital signs, procedures and perioperative events throughout a patient's surgical journey.

🤖 AI-Assisted Technology

Emerging artificial intelligence tools may support clinical monitoring, prediction and workflow analysis, although physician judgment remains central to patient care.

⚙️
Technology Note

New anesthesia technologies are intended to support—not replace—clinical decision-making. Their usefulness depends on evidence, system integration, physician training and the specific needs of each patient and procedure.

Anesthesiologist Practice Settings

Anesthesiologists can build careers in several healthcare environments, and each setting can provide a different mix of surgical exposure, patient populations, scheduling requirements and professional responsibilities. The practice model selected can also influence compensation and long-term career development.

🏥 Hospitals

Hospital-based Anesthesiologists support scheduled operations, emergency procedures, obstetric services, trauma care and other complex cases across multiple departments.

👥 Private Anesthesia Groups

Physician-owned and independent groups may provide opportunities for partnership, shared call coverage and compensation linked to group productivity or case volume.

🎓 Academic Medical Centers

Academic positions combine patient care with medical education, resident supervision, research and exposure to highly complex cases.

🏨 Ambulatory Surgery Centers

Anesthesiologists in outpatient surgical facilities commonly care for patients undergoing planned procedures that allow recovery and discharge without a prolonged hospital stay.

🏥
Practice Setting Note

Work schedules and compensation can differ significantly between practice environments. Physicians should evaluate clinical workload, call responsibilities, case complexity, benefits, partnership terms and geographic conditions when comparing opportunities.

Essential Skills for Anesthesiologists

Anesthesiology requires a combination of medical knowledge, technical ability and calm decision-making. Physicians must continuously evaluate patients and respond to changing clinical conditions while maintaining safe anesthesia throughout a procedure.

🧠 Clinical Judgment

Strong clinical reasoning helps Anesthesiologists evaluate risk, select appropriate anesthesia plans and respond to unexpected changes during procedures.

🫁 Airway Management

Competence in airway assessment and management is essential for maintaining adequate ventilation and oxygenation during anesthesia.

💉 Procedural Skills

Anesthesiologists use a range of technical procedures, including vascular access, airway techniques and regional anesthesia when clinically indicated.

⚡ Emergency Response

The ability to recognize deterioration and respond quickly is important when patients experience unexpected complications or changes in vital signs.

🤝 Team Communication

Effective communication with surgeons, nurses and other clinicians helps coordinate perioperative care and supports patient safety.

📊 Continuous Learning

Anesthesiologists must stay current with medications, monitoring methods, clinical evidence, equipment and evolving standards of care.

Frequently Asked Questions About Anesthesiologist Salaries in the USA

Find practical answers about Anesthesiologist salary levels in the USA, residency, subspecialties, employer compensation, private practice, state differences and long-term physician career opportunities.

What is the average Anesthesiologist salary in the USA?

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics provides an official occupational benchmark for Anesthesiologist wages, while physician compensation surveys may report different averages. Actual salary in the USA depends on experience, location, employer, workload, case mix and practice structure.

How much does an Anesthesiologist make per month in the USA?

Monthly Anesthesiologist pay in the USA can be estimated by dividing annual compensation by twelve. Actual monthly income may differ because physician contracts can include productivity bonuses, call compensation, incentives and other forms of additional pay.

Why are Anesthesiologists highly paid in the USA?

Anesthesiologists in the USA undergo extensive physician training and are responsible for anesthesia planning, airway management, physiological monitoring and rapid clinical decisions during surgical and procedural care.

How does residency affect an Anesthesiologist salary in the USA?

Anesthesiology residency is a supervised training period and compensation is much lower than attending physician earnings. After completing residency and meeting licensing requirements, physicians can enter independent practice with compensation based on their employment arrangement.

Do Anesthesiologists in the USA receive extra pay for call duty?

Some U.S. employers provide additional compensation for overnight, weekend or emergency call coverage. Other contracts include call responsibilities within the overall compensation package, so payment arrangements vary between employers.

Can private practice increase Anesthesiologist earnings in the USA?

Private anesthesia practices can offer partnership and productivity-based earning opportunities. However, physicians should also consider administrative responsibilities, practice expenses, ownership risks and case volume before comparing private practice with employed positions.

What Anesthesiology subspecialties can physicians pursue in the USA?

U.S. Anesthesiologists can pursue additional training in areas including cardiac anesthesiology, pediatric anesthesiology, critical care, pain medicine, obstetric anesthesiology and regional anesthesia.

Does an Anesthesiology fellowship lead to a higher salary in the USA?

Fellowship training can provide advanced clinical expertise and access to specialized positions, but it does not guarantee higher compensation. Salary remains influenced by employer demand, geographic market, experience and the responsibilities of the position.

Where do Anesthesiologists work in the USA?

Anesthesiologists may work in hospitals, academic medical centers, private physician groups, ambulatory surgery centers, pain clinics and other procedural healthcare environments throughout the USA.

How do ambulatory surgery centers affect Anesthesiologist careers?

Ambulatory surgery centers generally provide anesthesia services for scheduled outpatient procedures. These positions can offer different case mixes and scheduling patterns from large hospitals, making them an alternative career setting for U.S. Anesthesiologists.

Does state location affect Anesthesiologist salary in the USA?

Yes. Anesthesiologist compensation can vary across U.S. states and metropolitan areas because of physician supply, surgical demand, healthcare infrastructure, local living costs and competition among employers.

Is an Anesthesiologist salary different from other anesthesia providers?

Yes. Anesthesiologists are physicians who complete medical school and specialized anesthesiology residency training. Other anesthesia professionals follow separate educational and licensing pathways, so their compensation should not be combined with physician salary data.

Can international medical graduates become Anesthesiologists in the USA?

International medical graduates can pursue Anesthesiology careers in the USA after meeting applicable credentialing, examination, residency, certification and state medical licensing requirements. Eligibility can vary according to individual circumstances and state regulations.

Is Anesthesiology a good physician career in the USA?

Anesthesiology can suit physicians interested in procedural medicine, physiology, acute decision-making and perioperative care. The specialty also involves significant responsibility, continuous monitoring and the ability to respond quickly to changing patient conditions.

Will AI replace Anesthesiologists in the USA?

Artificial intelligence is more likely to support anesthesia workflows than replace physician-led care. Anesthesiologists remain responsible for evaluating patients, creating anesthesia plans, interpreting clinical changes and managing unexpected complications.
