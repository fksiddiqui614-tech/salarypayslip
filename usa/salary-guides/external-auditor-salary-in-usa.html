How External Auditor Salary Is Reported

External Auditor is not published as a separate national wage category in the current BLS occupational wage tables. External Auditing roles are included within Accountants and Auditors, SOC 13-2011.00. The May 2025 national median for this broader occupation was $83,680 per year, with a median hourly wage of $40.23.

This occupational benchmark covers accountants and different auditing roles, so it should not be treated as a guaranteed salary for every External Auditor. Actual earnings can change with audit firm, client responsibility, experience, credentials, specialization and location.

External Audit Roles & Specializations

External auditing includes financial statement assurance as well as specialized assignments involving particular industries, reporting areas and control environments. The exact work performed depends on the client, engagement scope and auditor's level of responsibility.

External Audit Specialty Typical Focus
Financial Statement Audit Examines financial statements, account balances and supporting evidence.
Public Company Audit Reviews financial reporting for organizations subject to public reporting requirements.
Private Company Audit Performs assurance procedures for privately held businesses and organizations.
Compliance Audit Reviews whether financial or operational activities follow applicable requirements.
IT Audit Examines technology systems, access controls, data processes and information systems.
Forensic & Investigative Audit Analyzes financial information and evidence related to suspected irregularities.

What Does an External Auditor Do?

External Auditors independently examine financial information and supporting evidence for client organizations. Their work helps provide assurance about financial reporting and can involve substantial communication with management and accounting teams.

  • Review financial statements and accounting records
  • Test selected transactions and account balances
  • Examine supporting documentation and audit evidence
  • Evaluate relevant internal controls
  • Perform audit sampling and analytical procedures
  • Document audit procedures and conclusions
  • Communicate audit requests with client teams
  • Identify unusual transactions or reporting risks
  • Review completed work performed by junior auditors
  • Prepare audit findings and engagement documentation

Modern External Auditors may also use data analytics, audit software, automated testing tools and cloud-based accounting systems to review larger volumes of financial information.

Future of External Auditing in the USA

External Audit practices are changing as accounting systems become more digital and audit teams gain access to larger datasets. Cloud accounting, automated testing, artificial intelligence and data analytics can change how evidence is selected, reviewed and documented.

As routine procedures become more automated, External Auditors may spend more time on professional judgment, risk assessment, complex accounting issues, unusual transactions and communication with clients.

Areas Increasingly Important for External Auditors

  • Audit data analytics
  • Continuous and automated testing
  • Artificial intelligence in audit workflows
  • Cybersecurity controls
  • Cloud accounting audits
  • Financial reporting analysis
  • Regulatory compliance
  • Fraud risk assessment
  • Technology controls
  • Digital evidence analysis

Technology is changing the tools used by accounting and audit professionals while increasing the importance of analytical skills, professional judgment and the ability to interpret complex financial information.
