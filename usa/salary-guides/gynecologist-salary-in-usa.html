Gynecologist Salary in USA Quick Answer

Gynecology is a specialized physician career focused on women's health, reproductive medicine and the diagnosis and treatment of gynecologic conditions. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reports wage information for Obstetricians and Gynecologists as a combined occupational category. In May 2025, this occupation had a mean annual wage of $279,040. Individual Gynecologist earnings can be higher or lower depending on experience, location, employer, patient workload, procedures and compensation structure.

BLS Mean Annual Wage
$279,040

Estimated Monthly Equivalent
≈ $23,253

This monthly figure is calculated by dividing the reported annual mean wage by twelve and should not be viewed as a guaranteed physician paycheck.

Hourly Equivalent
$134.16

BLS reported a mean hourly wage of $134.16 in May 2025 for the combined Obstetricians and Gynecologists occupational category.

Career Outlook
1% Growth

BLS projects employment for Obstetricians and Gynecologists to increase slightly from 2024 to 2034, with ongoing demand for specialized women's health services.

How to Become a Gynecologist in the USA

The path to becoming a practicing Gynecologist requires years of structured education, supervised clinical training, examinations and professional licensing. Students should view the process as a long-term medical career path rather than a short route to a high-paying occupation.

Students generally complete a bachelor's degree or appropriate undergraduate education before applying to medical school.

2️⃣ Earn an MD or DO

Medical school provides foundational education in anatomy, physiology, clinical medicine, patient care and medical decision-making.

3️⃣ Complete Residency

Graduates enter accredited Obstetrics and Gynecology residency training to develop supervised clinical and procedural skills.

4️⃣ Obtain Licensure

Physicians must satisfy applicable examination, credentialing and state licensing requirements before practicing independently.

Frequently Asked Questions About Gynecologist Salary and Careers in the USA

Find answers to common USA searches about Gynecologist careers, including education, residency, licensing, specialties, hospitals, private practice, employment settings, international pathways and professional growth.

What does a Gynecologist do in the USA?

A Gynecologist provides medical care related to women's reproductive health, including preventive services, diagnosis, treatment and management of reproductive-system conditions. Daily responsibilities depend on the physician's practice and specialty focus.

What is the difference between a Gynecologist and an OB-GYN in the USA?

Gynecology focuses primarily on women's reproductive health, while Obstetrics involves pregnancy, childbirth and postpartum care. An OB-GYN is trained to provide both obstetric and gynecologic services.

What degree do you need to become a Gynecologist in the USA?

Becoming a Gynecologist requires medical education followed by accredited postgraduate training in Obstetrics and Gynecology. Physicians must also satisfy the professional and licensing requirements applicable to the state where they intend to practice.

How long does it take to become a Gynecologist in the USA?

The pathway generally includes undergraduate education, medical school and an Obstetrics and Gynecology residency. Additional fellowship training may be completed by physicians who want to develop expertise in a particular subspecialty.

What is the Gynecology residency like in the USA?

Obstetrics and Gynecology residency combines clinical education with supervised patient care. Residents gain experience in areas such as women's health, reproductive medicine, obstetrics, gynecologic surgery and emergency care.

Is Gynecology competitive in the USA?

Gynecology is a specialized physician career requiring strong academic preparation, medical education and residency training. Competitiveness can vary by residency program, applicant background, location and the overall U.S. graduate medical education environment.

What specialties can a Gynecologist pursue in the USA?

Physicians can pursue additional training in areas such as gynecologic oncology, maternal-fetal medicine, reproductive endocrinology and infertility, female pelvic medicine and reconstructive surgery, and complex family planning.

Can a Gynecologist work without practicing Obstetrics in the USA?

Some physicians choose careers focused primarily on gynecology rather than providing obstetric services. Available roles depend on training, employer requirements, local patient demand and the physician's preferred scope of practice.

Where do Gynecologists work in the USA?

Gynecologists can work in hospitals, physician groups, outpatient clinics, private practices, academic medical centers, community healthcare organizations and other healthcare settings.

Can a Gynecologist work in a private practice in the USA?

Yes. Gynecologists may work as employed physicians, join established medical groups or participate in private practices. Practice arrangements vary according to state regulations, business structure and the physician's professional goals.

Can a Gynecologist own a medical practice in the USA?

Practice ownership may be possible depending on state law and the structure of the healthcare business. Physicians considering ownership should review professional licensing rules, corporate practice restrictions and applicable business requirements.

Do Gynecologists work in hospitals in the USA?

Many Gynecologists have hospital affiliations or work directly within hospital-based healthcare systems. Hospital responsibilities can include consultations, procedures, surgical care and coordination with other medical specialists.

What skills are important for a Gynecologist in the USA?

Important professional skills include patient communication, clinical decision-making, surgical ability, attention to detail, medical documentation, teamwork and the ability to manage sensitive patient-care situations professionally.

Can Gynecologists specialize in gynecologic surgery?

Yes. Gynecologists may perform a range of surgical procedures depending on their training, credentials, practice environment and scope of practice. Additional subspecialty education can provide further preparation for complex procedures.

What benefits do Gynecologists receive from U.S. employers?

Employment packages may include health insurance, retirement contributions, malpractice coverage, paid leave, continuing medical education support, licensing assistance and other professional benefits. The exact package depends on the employer and employment contract.

Can an international medical graduate become a Gynecologist in the USA?

International medical graduates may pursue U.S. Obstetrics and Gynecology careers after completing the applicable credentialing, examination, residency, certification and state licensing requirements. Previous overseas training does not automatically provide U.S. practice authorization.

Can an international Gynecologist practice in California?

Internationally trained physicians seeking to practice in California must meet the state's medical licensing requirements in addition to applicable U.S. education, examination and credentialing conditions. Requirements should be confirmed directly with the relevant California licensing authority.

Can an international Gynecologist practice in Texas?

International medical graduates may pursue medical careers in Texas after satisfying applicable credentialing, training and physician licensing requirements. The pathway depends on the physician's education, examination history, postgraduate training and professional circumstances.

Do Gynecologists need board certification in the USA?

Board certification is an important professional credential for many physicians and can be relevant to employment, hospital privileges and professional development. Certification requirements depend on the physician's specialty and the applicable certifying organization.

What is the job outlook for Gynecologists in the USA?

Career opportunities for Gynecologists are influenced by women's healthcare needs, physician workforce trends, population changes, regional demand and healthcare access. Opportunities can differ between metropolitan areas, rural communities, hospitals and private practices.

Is Gynecology a good career in the USA?

Gynecology can be a rewarding medical career for physicians interested in women's health, preventive care, diagnosis, treatment and long-term patient relationships. The profession requires extensive education and training but offers several clinical and professional pathways.
