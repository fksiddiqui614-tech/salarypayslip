What Affects Orthodontist Pay in Alabama?

Several factors can influence how much an Orthodontist earns in Alabama. Experience is important, but compensation can also depend on the type of practice, patient volume, location and whether the orthodontist is an employee, associate or practice owner.

Practice Type

Private orthodontic practices, dental groups and healthcare organizations can have different compensation structures. Some positions combine a base salary with production incentives or other benefits.

Experience and Clinical Skills

Orthodontists with more clinical experience and a strong patient base may have greater earning potential. Specialized treatment skills and the ability to manage complex cases can also affect compensation.

Practice Ownership

Practice owners have a different income structure from employed orthodontists. Revenue, operating costs, staffing, equipment, marketing and patient volume all influence owner earnings.
